The short story

Made to last, sold at an honest price.

We started Donkey with one rule: only sell things we would keep in our own homes. That means everyday pieces that hold up, from people we know by name.

We look for small makers who care about how a thing wears in, not how fast it sells. When something runs out, we say so; we would rather be honest than busy.

Everything ships from our shop on Mill Lane, packed by the same people who chose it.
