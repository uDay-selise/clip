### PG-03 Reach Donkey
**What it is**
A contact page showing how to reach Donkey: an email address and a phone number, plus any address, kept up to date by the team. Shoppers use it before or after they head into the shop.

**Who can use it**
- Shopper: Open the page and see Donkey's contact details.
- Editor: Change the email, phone number and address in the editors' area.

**Where it lives**
- Contact page, at /contact
- Link labelled Contact in the top menu

**How it works**
1. A shopper clicks "Contact" in the top menu.
2. The page shows the email address as a clickable link that opens their mail app.
3. It shows the phone number as a clickable link that starts a call on a phone.
4. When the team has written an address, it shows below the phone number.
5. Below the contact details, a short form lets a shopper send a name, email and message, which the team reads under Form entries in the editors' area.

**Rules**
- The email address and phone number are required; the address is optional.
- Each detail shows only while the team has filled it in.

**What people see (exact words)**
- Empty: ""Contact details are on their way. Check back soon." This shows only when both the email and the phone number are blank."

**What it keeps**
- PageWording: pageKey, heading, intro (already in the project, used as it is). Who may: Visitors read the contact details; Editors change them in the editors' area. Heading and intro are kept by Donkey app; the contact details are this site's own page wording.

**Built with (Blocks)**
- public records: Shows the contact details the team keeps, so shoppers read them without signing in.

**Not included**
- No contact form on this page; shoppers reach Donkey by email or phone.

**Acceptance checks**
- PG-03 AC1: Given the Editor sets the phone number to "01234 567890", when a shopper opens Contact, then the phone number shows as a clickable "01234 567890" link.
- PG-03 AC2: Given both the email and the phone number are blank, when a shopper opens Contact, then they see "Contact details are on their way. Check back soon."
