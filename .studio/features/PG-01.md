### PG-01 See the home page
**What it is**
The first page anyone opens. It says what Donkey sells and gives the way into the shop, with the Sign up button at the top and in the middle of the page. The words on it come from the designers until the team changes them.

**Who can use it**
- Shopper: Open the home page and use the Sign up button.
- Editor: Change the home page wording and picture in the editors' area.

**Where it lives**
- Home page, at the site's main address
- Link labelled Home in the top menu

**How it works**
1. A shopper opens the site's address and lands on the home page.
2. The top bar shows the Donkey name, links to Home, How it works, Contact and Products, and a Sign up button.
3. The page opens with a headline and a short line saying what Donkey sells.
4. Below that, a short block explains what shoppers can do here, and a Sign up button labelled "Sign up" leads into Donkey app.
5. A shopper clicks "Sign up" and is taken to Donkey app's sign-up screen.

**Other path: Already has an account**
1. The shopper clicks "Sign in" in the top bar.
2. They are taken to Donkey app's sign-in screen.

**Rules**
- The Sign up button leads to Donkey app, never to a sign-up form on this site.
- Shoppers read the home page without signing in; nothing on the home page is hidden.
- Prices shown anywhere on this site are in pounds, the bare price only, for example "£12.50".
- The Sign in button opens the project's hosted sign-in page, the same one Donkey app uses; there is no sign-in form on this site.

**What people see (exact words)**
- Empty: "The home page always shows its headline and Sign up button; there is no empty state."

**What it keeps**
- PageWording: pageKey (required, fixed to the home page), heading (required, up to 120 characters), intro (up to 300 characters). Who may: Visitors read the home page wording; Editors change it in the editors' area. It is kept by Donkey app.

**Built with (Blocks)**
- public records: Shows the home page wording the team keeps, so shoppers read it without signing in.

**Not included**
- No product list on the home page; products have their own section on the site.
- No sign-up or sign-in form on this site; both buttons lead to Donkey app.

**Acceptance checks**
- PG-01 AC1: Given a shopper opens the site's address, when the home page loads, then they see the headline, a line saying what Donkey sells, and a "Sign up" button.
- PG-01 AC2: Given a shopper on the home page, when they click "Sign up", then Donkey app's sign-up screen opens.
