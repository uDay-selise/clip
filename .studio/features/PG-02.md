### PG-02 See how buying works
**What it is**
One page that explains how buying from Donkey works, from picking a product to it arriving. It carries the delivery and returns details the team keeps up to date, and a shopper reads it before heading into the shop.

**Who can use it**
- Shopper: Open the page and read it without signing in.
- Editor: Change the delivery and returns text in the editors' area.

**Where it lives**
- How it works page, at /how-it-works
- Link labelled How it works in the top menu

**How it works**
1. A shopper clicks "How it works" in the top menu.
2. The page shows three short steps: pick what you want, pay at the checkout, and get it delivered.
3. Below the steps, a Delivery section shows the delivery text the team keeps.
4. Below that, a Returns section shows the returns text the team keeps.
5. At the foot of the page, a "Sign up" button leads into Donkey app.

**Rules**
- The delivery and returns text is required and can be up to 1,000 characters each.
- The page shows the delivery and returns text as the team wrote it, with no editing by anyone else.

**What people see (exact words)**
- Empty: "Delivery: "We'll add our delivery details here soon." Returns: "We'll add our returns details here soon.""

**What it keeps**
- PageWording: pageKey, heading, intro (already in the project, used as it is). Who may: Visitors read the page; Editors change it in the editors' area. Heading and intro are kept by Donkey app; deliveryText and returnsText are this site's own page wording.

**Built with (Blocks)**
- public records: Shows the delivery and returns text the team keeps, so shoppers read it without signing in.

**Not included**
- No per-product delivery times or charges; one delivery text covers the whole shop.

**Acceptance checks**
- PG-02 AC1: Given the Editor writes "Orders arrive in 3 to 5 days." in the delivery text, when a shopper opens How it works, then the Delivery section reads "Orders arrive in 3 to 5 days."
- PG-02 AC2: Given no delivery text has been written yet, when a shopper opens How it works, then the Delivery section reads "We'll add our delivery details here soon."
