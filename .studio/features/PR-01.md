### PR-01 Browse the products and prices
**What it is**
The products page where shoppers see each thing Donkey sells, with a short description and what it costs. Prices show in pounds, the bare price only. The products and their prices are the ones the team keeps in the editors' area.

**Who can use it**
- Shopper: See every product marked visible, with its description and price.
- Editor: Add, change and remove products and their photos in the editors' area.

**Where it lives**
- Products page, at /products
- Link labelled Products in the top menu

**How it works**
1. A shopper clicks "Products" in the top menu.
2. The page lists the products the team has marked visible, in the order the team set.
3. Each product shows its photo, its name, its description and its price in pounds.
4. At the foot of each product a "Sign up" button leads into Donkey app, where the shopper can buy it.

**Rules**
- A product shows only while it is marked visible.
- A price shows in pounds with two decimals and a pound sign, for example "£12.50"; the bare price only, with no currency words or notes beside it.
- A price of 1250 pence shows as "£12.50": a dot goes before the last two digits.
- A product with no description shows its name and price alone.
- When the team wrote a medicine note for a product, it shows directly under that product's price.

**What people see (exact words)**
- Empty: ""Our products are on their way. Check back soon.""
- Not allowed: "A product marked not visible never appears; shoppers are not told it exists."

**What it keeps**
- Product: name (required), description (up to 500 characters), pricePence (required, whole pence, at least 0), group (optional), medicineNote (optional), visible (yes/no, off by default), photoFileId (optional image), sortOrder (whole number, lowest first). Who may: Visitors read the products marked visible; Editors add, change and remove products, and choose which are visible. It is kept by Donkey app.

**Built with (Blocks)**
- public records: Shows the products and prices the team publishes, so shoppers read them without signing in.

**Not included**
- No buying, cart or checkout on this site; the Sign up button leads into Donkey app for that.
- No filters or search yet; the whole list shows in the team's order.

**Acceptance checks**
- PR-01 AC1: Given a product with the price 1250 pence is published, when a shopper opens the products page, then it shows "£12.50" and no other price wording.
- PR-01 AC2: Given a product is not marked visible, when a shopper opens the products page, then that product does not appear.
- PR-01 AC3: Given no products are marked visible, when a shopper opens the products page, then they see "Our products are on their way. Check back soon."

Status: New in Plan 1.
