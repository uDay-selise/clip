### ED-01 Edit the site's content
**What it is**
The area where the Donkey team signs in to keep the site up to date: the shop's page wording, the delivery and returns text, the contact details, and every product with its price and photo. It lives at its own address, /editor, which visitors never see or reach. Editors are invited by email and are never shoppers or customers.

**Who can use it**
- Editor: Sign in at /editor and add, change and remove page wording, products and their photos, and read what forms sent.
- Shopper: Never sign in and never reach /editor.

**Where it lives**
- Editors' area at /editor
- Sign-in page reached from /editor
- Products list and Page wording menu inside /editor

**How it works**
1. An editor opens /editor at the app's address, which is not linked from any visitor page.
2. They sign in with their email address and password on the sign-in page; a first-time editor sets the password from the email they were sent.
3. They land on the editors' area: a menu with Products and Page wording.
4. Under Page wording they pick Home, How it works or Contact and change the heading and intro there; the How it works page also holds the delivery and returns text, and the Contact page also holds the email, phone number and address, then they click "Save".
5. Under Products they click "Add product", fill in the name, description, price, group, medicine note, photo, visible switch and order, then click "Save".
6. An existing product is opened, changed and saved, or removed with "Remove", which asks "Remove this product from the site?" before it goes.
7. An existing editor adds a new editor by typing their email address and sending the invite, which gives them the Editor role and emails them a link to set their password.

**Rules**
- The editors' area lives at /editor and is never linked from a visitor page.
- Only people invited as editors can sign in there; nobody signs themselves up.
- Inviting an editor sends them an email with a link to set their password, which needs the project's email set up once.
- Every product needs a name and a price before it can be saved.
- A product only appears on the site while its visible switch is on.

**What people see (exact words)**
- Empty: "Products: "No products yet. Add your first one." Form entries: "No entries yet.""
- Not allowed: "Someone without an editor invite sees the sign-in page and cannot get further."
- Saved: ""Saved. Shoppers see this now.""

**What it keeps**
- Editor account: editor's name, editor's email address, role (Editor). Who may: Editors see and change everything in the editors' area. Shoppers and other visitors never reach it. It is kept by Studio.
- PageWording: pageKey, heading, intro (already in the project, used as it is). Who may: Editors add, change and remove the wording of every page; visitors read it on the pages. Kept by Donkey app.
- Product: name, description, pricePence, group, medicineNote, visible, photoFileId, sortOrder. Who may: Editors add, change and remove products; visitors read those marked visible. Kept by Donkey app.

**Built with (Blocks)**
- sign-in: Lets each editor sign in on the hosted sign-in page before they reach /editor.
- records: Keeps the editors' area's own editor accounts and their role.
- public records: Keeps the page wording and products the editors change, which visitors then read.
- roles: Only people with the Editor role may open /editor and change anything.
- invitations: The owner invites each editor by email to set their password. Needs from the owner: The project's email set up once, so invite emails can go out.
- files: Keeps the product photos editors upload.

**Not included**
- No visitor or customer ever gets an editor account; there is no sign-up for editors.
- No page for choosing colours or the look of the site.

**Acceptance checks**
- ED-01 AC1: Given an invited editor opens /editor, when they sign in with their emailed link, then they see the editors' area with Products, Page wording and Form entries.
- ED-01 AC2: Given a shopper on any visitor page, when they look at the top bar and footer, then there is no link to /editor.
- ED-01 AC3: Given an editor changes a product's price to 1250 pence and saves, when a shopper opens /products, then that product shows "£12.50".
